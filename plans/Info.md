# Cloud-based HR and payroll application suite 

B2B (Business-to-Business) Cloud Applications or Software as a Service (SaaS). Within the HR industry, they are specifically known by several technical names:

- HRMS (Human Resource Management System): A sophisticated suite that combines core employee data tracking with administrative tools like payroll and performance management.

- HRIS (Human Resource Information System): A system focused on employee record management, data organization, and reporting.

- HCM (Human Capital Management): A broader term for platforms that manage the entire employee lifecycle, including recruitment, training, and strategic workforce planning.

- Web Portal / Employee Self-Service (ESS): A secure online entry point where employees can personally access payslips, tax forms, and leave requests.
